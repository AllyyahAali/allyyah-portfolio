A little about me

The through line is people.

I’m Allyyah Aali, a fundraising and events coordinator at Associated Black Charities. I work across experience design, outreach, communications, and the details that make an idea real.

Before this chapter, I served as Towson University’s Student Government Association president, representing a student body of more than 20,000 and leading a 70-plus-member organization. That experience still shapes how I listen, organize, and bring people together.
