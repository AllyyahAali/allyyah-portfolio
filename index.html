01 / EXPERIENCE DESIGN

Associated Black Charities · In development

Equity in Action 2026

Making a full-day equity conference feel intentional at every touchpoint.

03 / COMMUNITY EVENT

Associated Black Charities · September 2026

The Cookout 2026

A festival celebration of Black culture that brought resources, employers, families, and community partners together at the Pikesville Armory.

My contribution Coordinated vendor and activation details, followed up on food permitting, prepared vendor and attendee communications, and developed run-of-show and on-site materials. After the event, I worked on the visual recap using real event photography.

Event impact ABC reported more than 900 attendees, over 20 workforce providers, and over 30 community resource vendors. Presenting sponsor SECU reported 84 new accounts opened on site.

These figures describe the ABC team’s event-wide results, not outcomes attributable to one person.

A little about me

The through line is people.

I’m Allyyah Aali, a Baltimore-based fundraising and events coordinator at Associated Black Charities. I bring together event production, strategic communications, community partnerships, and the small operational choices that shape how people experience an organization.

My background in mass communications taught me to ask who a message is for and what it should move them to do. My work in fundraising and events has made that question tangible: a guest finding the right resource, a company bringing its team into a conversation about equity, or a supporter seeing a clear way to stay involved.

At Towson University, I served as Student Government Association president, representing more than 20,000 students and leading a 70-plus-member organization. I learned to listen across different needs, speak with clarity, and turn ideas into coordinated action. I carry that approach into every project.

I'm drawn to the space where culture, public purpose, storytelling, and live experiences meet. I care about work that looks thoughtful, runs well, and leaves people feeling more connected than when they arrived.
